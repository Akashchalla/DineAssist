Built with

Python · OpenCV · YOLO · FastAPI · SQLite · Streamlit

The full local application supports video and RTSP input, manual staff labels, recorded events, a waitlist, and host-triggered phone notifications. This Vercel site is a standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript evaluation demo, with no server or camera connection.

What comes next

Real restaurant testing, more reliable staff identification, and validated reporting on greeting delays and table turnaround. Predicting departure times and peak-hour reports are future work.
