Built with

Python · OpenCV · YOLO26 · FastAPI · SQLite · Streamlit

The full local application supports video and RTSP input, manual staff labels, recorded events, a waitlist, and host-triggered phone notifications. This Vercel site is a standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript evaluation demo, with no server or camera connection.

Why YOLO26

YOLO26 was released on January 14, 2026, within the past year. It is designed for edge devices and drops the separate NMS post-processing step. On our sample video it ran about 15% faster than YOLO11 on a CPU and matched people to 10 of 14 tables as reliably; it found people at the smallest, farthest tables less often, so we lowered the confidence threshold to 0.25.

What comes next

Real restaurant testing, more reliable staff identification, and validated reporting on greeting delays and table turnaround. Predicting departure times and peak-hour reports are future work.
